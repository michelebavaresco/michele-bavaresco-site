about mesobre mim

Hi, I am a yoga and mindfulness facilitator based in Portugal. I create spaces where you can slow down, reconnect, and feel at home in your body - whether through movement, stillness, or shared experiences. My approach blends strength and softness, presence and depth - supporting you not just in your practice, but in how you move through life.

Sou facilitadora de yoga e mindfulness em Portugal. Crio espaços onde você pode desacelerar, se reconectar e sentir-se em casa no próprio corpo — através do movimento, da presença e de experiências compartilhadas.
