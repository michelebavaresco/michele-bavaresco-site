the path so far

When I sit on my mat, I feel in service to something greater than myself. In those moments, I am a drop in the vast ocean – unique, yet inseparable from the whole.

To me, yoga is not a trend or a workout. It is therapy, philosophy, and a way of life. Yoga is the mind in motion – as sacred as a church to a priest, as vital as the ocean to a surfer. It is the science of the mind, the key to understanding contradictions, dualities, and the codes of life itself.

Yoga healed me, and it continues to teach me who I am. It is medicine, a process of growth, an endless invitation to awaken vitality and purpose. These ancient teachings are not bound by time – they invite us to rethink, to reestablish our essence, and to return to the truth of who we are.

Yoga has completely changed my life. And I know it can change the lives of those who are ready to open their hearts to it.

movement as meditation

A gentle weave of Hatha, Meditation, Kriyas and Yoga Nidra, adapted to the moment.

1×1: personalized sessions for your body and rhythm. Group sessions: gentle community practice, all levels welcome. Retreats: immersive time to slow down and renew. Yoga Nidra is conscious rest — where body and mind soften and the being awakens to its natural clarity.

the journey

I offer spaces where movement becomes meditation — an invitation to ground, release, and rise. Rooted in the lineage of Hatha Yoga, my teachings weave together traditional wisdom and embodied presence, guiding each practitioner toward a deeper connection with themselves.

With over 800 hours of certified training (RYT-800), my path integrates a blend of practices and sādhanas, including Cosmic Yoga, Vinyasa, Ashtanga, Pranayama, and Vipassana Meditation as taught by S. N. Goenka. Each class is designed as a journey — honouring the body as a temple, the breath as a bridge, and awareness as the source of transformation.
