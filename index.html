about me

I’m Michele, a yoga and mindfulness facilitator based in Portugal. I create spaces where you can slow down, reconnect, and feel at home in your body - whether through movement, stillness, or shared experiences. My approach blends strength and softness, presence and depth - supporting you not just in your practice, but in how you move through life.

how would you like to practice or collaborate

host & space holder for retreats & events

If you’re creating a retreat, immersion, or meaningful gathering and need someone to hold the space with presence and care, I’m here to support you.

I collaborate with brands, facilitators, and creators to co-create experiences that feel intentional, grounded, and deeply human.

From wellness retreats to private events and conscious gatherings, I help shape an experience where people can truly slow down, connect, and feel.
